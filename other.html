pLaTeX

Aug 2026 – Present

A LaTeX speed-typesetting game. A real TeX Live 2025 engine, compiled to WebAssembly, runs in the browser: both the target and whatever you type are compiled to PDF, rasterised at the same scale, and compared pixel for pixel. The upshot is that anything which typesets identically is accepted, so \textbf{x} and {\bfseries x} both pass. Three modes: an untimed 48-exercise curriculum, a three-minute Blaze run over a separate 100-problem catalog, and a Fix-it mode that hands you broken source to repair.

DeepLOB-lite: Predicting Stock Mid-Price Movements

Sept 2025

A DeepLOB-inspired stack (CNN, Inception module, LSTM) predicting mid-price movement from the FI-2010 limit order book dataset, 40 features across 100 timesteps. Reached roughly 78% accuracy and 77% F1 against logistic and ridge regression baselines, with LIME attribution to identify which order book levels actually drive a prediction, and a simple backtest of the resulting signal.
